# Officer Management

Manage officer positions and assignments at the national (Grand Chapter) level.

## Overview

The officer management system allows administrators to:

* View all officer titles and current assignments
* Assign members to officer positions
* Remove officer assignments
* Search for members to fill positions

## Accessing Officer Management

1. Navigate to **Grand Chapter Settings** → **Officer Management**

## Officer Management Screen

The screen has two tabs:

### Officers Tab

Displays current officer assignments grouped by title:

* Each title section shows:
  * **Title name** (e.g., Grand Polemarch, Polemarch, Vice Polemarch)
  * **Display order** - Titles are shown in organizational hierarchy order
  * **Current officer(s)** - Name, photo, email, and chapter
  * **Allows multiple** - Some titles allow multiple officers (e.g., Board Members)

### Titles Tab

Shows all available officer titles:

* Title name
* Display order
* Whether the title allows multiple officers

## Assigning an Officer

1. Go to the **Officers** tab
2. Find the title you want to fill
3. Tap **Add Officer** (or the + button next to the title)
4. Search for a member by name
5. Select the member from the results
6. Optionally add notes about the assignment
7. Confirm the assignment

The member is immediately assigned to the officer position.

## Removing an Officer

1. Go to the **Officers** tab
2. Find the officer you want to remove
3. Tap the remove or delete option
4. Confirm the removal

The officer assignment is removed immediately.

## Officer Information

Each officer entry displays:

* **Name** and profile photo
* **Email address**
* **Chapter affiliation**
* **Assignment date**
* **Notes** (if any were added during assignment)

## Officer Title Hierarchy

Officer titles have a defined display order that reflects the organizational hierarchy. Common titles include:

**National Level:**
* Grand Polemarch
* Grand Board Members
* National Officers

**Province Level:**
* Province Polemarch
* Province Board Members
* Province Officers

**Chapter Level:**
* Polemarch
* Vice Polemarch
* Keeper of Records
* Keeper of Exchequer
* Strategus
* Lieutenant Strategus
* Board of Directors

## Search Functionality

The search bar allows you to filter officers by name, making it easy to find specific assignments in organizations with many officer positions.
