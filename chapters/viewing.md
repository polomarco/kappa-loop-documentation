# Viewing Chapter Details

The chapter detail screen provides comprehensive information about a chapter, its officers, and members.

## Accessing Chapter Details

You can reach a chapter's detail screen by:

* Tapping a chapter from **Browse Chapters**
* Navigating to your chapter via the **FAB** → **Chapter**

## Chapter Information

The chapter detail screen displays:

### Basic Information

* **Chapter name**
* **Chapter type** (Alumni or Undergraduate)
* **Charter date** - When the chapter was chartered
* **Region/Province** the chapter belongs to
* **Chapter status** (Active/Inactive)

### School Information _(Undergraduate Chapters)_

* **School name** - The associated college or university

### Chapter Details

* **Number of members**
* **Chapter size** classification
* **Chapter website** (if available, tappable link)
* **Chapter address**
* **EIN number** (for tax purposes)
* **Insurance status** - Whether chapter insurance is paid

### Chapter Advisor

If the chapter has an assigned advisor:

* Advisor name
* Advisor photo

## Chapter Officers

Tap the **Officers** section to expand and view the chapter's leadership:

* Officers are grouped by title (e.g., Polemarch, Vice Polemarch, Keeper of Records)
* Each officer entry shows:
  * Name and photo
  * Officer title
  * Chapter name
* Tap an officer to view their profile

## Membership Status

The chapter detail screen shows your relationship with the chapter:

* **Member** - You are an active member of this chapter
* **Pending Request** - You have a pending membership request
* **Join** - Button to request membership (if not already a member)

## Joining the Chapter

If you're not a member, a **Join Chapter** button appears. See [Joining a Chapter](joining.md) for the full process.

## Viewing Chapter Initiates

If available, you can view the chapter's initiates:

* Tap the **Initiates** option to see a list
* Shows initiate information for the chapter

## Viewing Member Profiles

Tap any officer or member name to view their full profile. This navigates to their profile screen showing the information they've made visible based on their privacy settings.
