# Browsing Chapters

The Chapter Browse screen provides a searchable, filterable directory of all chapters in the fraternity.

## Accessing Chapter Browse

1. Tap the **menu icon** (☰) in the top-left corner
2. Select **Browse Chapters** from the drawer menu

## Searching for Chapters

Use the search bar at the top of the screen to search by:

* Chapter name
* School name (for undergraduate chapters)
* Location (city, state)

Search results update as you type.

## Filtering Chapters

Tap the filter icon to narrow your results by:

### Chapter Type
* Alumni Chapter
* Undergraduate Chapter

### Chapter Status
* Active
* Inactive

### Region/Province
* Select a specific province to view only chapters in that region

Filters can be combined. Tap **Clear** to remove all filters.

## Chapter List

Each chapter in the results shows:

* **Chapter name**
* **Chapter type** (Alumni/Undergraduate)
* **Region/Province**
* **School name** (for undergraduate chapters)
* **Location** (city, state)
* **Number of members**
* **Chapter status** (Active/Inactive)

## Pagination

Results are loaded in pages. Scroll to the bottom to load more chapters.

## Viewing Chapter Details

Tap any chapter card to open the full chapter detail screen. See [Viewing Chapter Details](viewing.md) for more information.
