# Organizational Settings

Configure settings for the Grand Chapter, province, and chapter levels.

## Accessing Settings

Settings are accessible from each organizational screen:

* **Grand Chapter** - Tap the settings/gear icon on the national screen
* **Province** - Tap the settings/gear icon on the province screen
* **Chapter** - Tap the settings/gear icon on the chapter screen

Settings options are only visible to users with administrative permissions at the corresponding level.

## Grand Chapter Settings

National administrators can access:

* **Officer Management** - Manage national officer assignments
* **National Role Assignment** - Grant national-level management roles
* **About** - App information, version, and support links

## Province Settings

Province administrators can access:

* **Officer Management** - Manage province officer assignments
* **Province Role Assignment** - Grant province-level management roles
* **About** - App information, version, and support links

## Chapter Settings

Chapter administrators can access:

* **Officer Management** - Manage chapter officer assignments via **Chapter Role Assignment**
* **Membership Requests** - Review and approve chapter membership requests (with pending count badge)
* **About** - App information, version, and support links

## About Screen

The About screen is accessible from all settings levels and provides:

* **App Information** - App name, version number, and description
* **App User Guide** - Link to the documentation (docs.kappaloop.com)
* **Help Desk** - Link to submit a support request
* **Privacy Policy** - Link to the privacy policy

## Sign Out

All settings screens include a **Sign Out** option to log out of the app. Signing out:

* Clears your session
* Returns you to the login screen
* Does not delete your account or data
