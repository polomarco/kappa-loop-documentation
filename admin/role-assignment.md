# Role Assignment

Manage organizational roles and permissions for content management at the national, province, and chapter levels.

## Overview

Role assignment allows administrators to grant specific management permissions to members, enabling them to create and manage content within the app.

## Accessing Role Assignment

Role assignment is accessible from the settings screen at each organizational level:

* **Manage Grand Chapter** → **Role Assignment** (National roles)
* **Manage Province** → **Role Assignment** (Province roles)
* **Manage Chapter** → **Role Assignment** (Chapter roles)

## Available Roles

Roles determine what actions a member can perform. Common management roles include:

### Content Management Roles

* **Announcement Manager** - Can create, edit, and delete announcements at the assigned scope
* **Event Manager** - Can create, edit, and delete events at the assigned scope
* **Vote Manager** - Can create and manage votes and polls at the assigned scope

### Administrative Roles

* **Settings Manager** - Can access and modify organizational settings
* **Officer Manager** - Can assign and remove officers
* **Member Manager** - Can manage chapter membership and requests

## Assigning a Role

1. Navigate to the **Role Assignment** screen for the appropriate level
2. Tap **Add Role** or the + button
3. Search for a member by name
4. Select the member
5. Choose the role(s) to assign
6. Confirm the assignment

## Removing a Role

1. Navigate to the **Role Assignment** screen
2. Find the member whose role you want to remove
3. Tap the remove option
4. Confirm the removal

The member's permissions are updated immediately.

## Scope of Roles

Roles are scoped to the organizational level where they are assigned:

* **National roles** - Apply to Grand Chapter content and settings
* **Province roles** - Apply to the specific province's content and settings
* **Chapter roles** - Apply to the specific chapter's content and settings

A member can hold roles at multiple levels simultaneously. For example, a member could be an Announcement Manager at both the chapter and province levels.

## Role Effects

When a role is assigned, the member gains access to:

* Relevant menu items in the drawer (e.g., "Create Announcement" appears for Announcement Managers)
* Management buttons on content screens (e.g., Edit and Delete buttons on announcements)
* Settings screens appropriate to their role level
* Draft content visibility for their assigned scope

## Best Practices

* Assign roles to the minimum number of members needed
* Review role assignments periodically and remove inactive officers
* Ensure at least one backup manager exists for each critical role
* When chapter leadership changes, update role assignments promptly
