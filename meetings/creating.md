# Creating Meetings _(For Managers)_

This guide explains how to schedule and manage chapter and province meetings.

## Who Can Create Meetings

* **Chapter Officers** - Can create chapter meetings
* **Province Officers** - Can create province meetings

## Accessing the Create Meeting Screen

1. Navigate to your **Chapter** or **Province** screen
2. Tap the **+** (plus) button or **Create Meeting** from the menu
3. The meeting creation form opens

## Meeting Creation Form

### Basic Information

* **Title** _(required)_ - A clear name for the meeting (e.g., "Monthly Chapter Meeting", "Executive Board Session")
* **Description** _(optional)_ - Additional details, agenda overview, or notes for attendees

### Date & Time

* **Date** _(required)_ - Select the meeting date
* **Start Time** _(required)_ - When the meeting begins
* **End Time** _(required)_ - When the meeting is scheduled to end
* **Timezone** - Automatically set based on your device

### Meeting Format

Choose one of three formats:

**Virtual:**
* **Meeting Platform** - Select from Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Other
* **Meeting Link** _(required)_ - The URL to join the virtual meeting
* **Meeting ID** _(optional)_ - Platform-specific meeting ID
* **Passcode** _(optional)_ - Meeting passcode for entry

**In-Person:**
* **Location Name** _(required)_ - Name of the venue (e.g., "Chapter House", "Community Center")
* **Street Address** - Full address with autocomplete support
* **Room Number** _(optional)_ - Specific room or suite number

**Hybrid:**
* All virtual fields (meeting link, platform, etc.)
* All in-person fields (location, address, etc.)

### Invitation Scope

Control who can see and attend the meeting:

* **All Chapter Members** - Every chapter member is invited
* **Chapter Officers Only** - Only officers receive the invitation
* **Specific Members** - Select individual members to invite

For **Specific Members**, a search interface allows you to find and add members by name.

### Attachments

Upload meeting materials during creation:

* Tap **Add Attachment** to select files from your device
* Supported formats: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images
* Files are uploaded when the meeting is saved

### Recording Settings

* Toggle **Recording Enabled** to indicate the meeting will be recorded
* Recording URLs can be added after the meeting

## Saving the Meeting

After filling in all required fields:

1. Tap **Save** or **Create Meeting**
2. The meeting is created with "Scheduled" status
3. Invited members can see the meeting in their chapter/province screen

## Editing a Meeting

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **Edit**
3. Modify any fields
4. Tap **Save** to update

Editing is available for meetings in any status. Changes to upcoming meetings are reflected immediately for all invited members.

## Cancelling a Meeting

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **Cancel Meeting**
3. Optionally provide a **cancellation reason**
4. Confirm the cancellation

Cancelled meetings remain visible with a "Cancelled" status and the cancellation reason.

## Best Practices

* Include a clear description with the agenda or key discussion points
* For virtual meetings, test the meeting link before sharing
* Set the correct timezone to avoid confusion for members in different areas
* Upload agendas and pre-read materials before the meeting
* Use the "Chapter Officers Only" scope for executive board meetings
* Enable recording for important meetings so absent members can catch up

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Create Meeting

Verify that you have officer or manager permissions for the chapter or province.

### Meeting Link Not Working

Ensure the full URL is entered including "https://". Test the link in a browser before saving.

### Attendees Can't See the Meeting

Check the invitation scope setting. If set to "Specific Members", ensure the intended attendees are added to the invite list.
