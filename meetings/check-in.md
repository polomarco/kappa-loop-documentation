# Meeting Check-in

The meeting check-in system provides digital attendance tracking for chapter and province meetings.

## Overview

Meeting check-in allows:

* **Attendees** to mark their presence at a meeting
* **Meeting organizers** to track who attended
* **Automatic recording** of check-in and check-out times
* **Integration with voting** - Meeting check-in can be required for vote eligibility

## Check-in Process

### For Attendees

1. Open the meeting from your chapter or province screen
2. Tap the **Check In** button on the meeting detail screen
3. Your attendance is recorded with the current timestamp
4. Your status changes to "Present"

### For Meeting Organizers

Meeting organizers can check in attendees manually:

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **Check In** to open the check-in management screen
3. Search for members by name
4. Tap a member to check them in
5. Their attendance status and time are recorded

## Attendance Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Present** | Member has checked in and is attending |
| **Absent** | Member did not check in |
| **Excused** | Member was excused from attending |
| **Late** | Member checked in after the meeting started |

## Attendee Roles

Each attendee can have a role assigned:

* **Host** - The primary meeting organizer
* **Co-Host** - Assists the host with meeting management
* **Presenter** - Giving a presentation during the meeting
* **Attendee** - Regular meeting participant
* **Guest** - Visiting member from another chapter

## Viewing Attendees

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **View Attendees** or switch to the attendee list
3. See all attendees with their:
   * Name and photo
   * Membership number
   * Chapter affiliation
   * Attendance status
   * Check-in time
   * Meeting role

### Visitor Identification

Members from other chapters are identified as visitors, showing their home chapter name.

## Check-out

When leaving a meeting:

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **Check Out**
3. Your check-out time is recorded

## Exporting Attendance

Meeting organizers can export attendance records:

1. Open the meeting detail screen
2. Tap **Export Attendance** (or the export icon)
3. The attendance list is generated for download

The export includes member names, membership numbers, check-in times, check-out times, and attendance status.

## Integration with Voting

Meeting check-in can be used as an eligibility requirement for votes:

* When creating a vote, managers can require check-in to a specific meeting
* Only members who checked into that meeting will be eligible to vote
* This ensures that only present members participate in meeting-related votes

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Check In

* The meeting must be in "In Progress" or "Scheduled" status
* You must be an invited attendee (check the meeting's invitation scope)
* Ensure you have an internet connection

### Check-in Time is Wrong

Check-in times are based on your device's current time and timezone settings. Ensure your device's date and time are set correctly.

### Missing from Attendee List

If you checked in but don't appear on the list, pull down to refresh. If the issue persists, ask the meeting organizer to manually check you in.
