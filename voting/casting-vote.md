# Casting a Vote

This guide explains how to submit your vote on active ballots and polls.

## Prerequisites

Before you can vote:

* The vote must be in **Active** status
* The voting period must have started
* You must meet all **eligibility requirements** for the vote
* You must not have already voted (each member can only vote once per vote)

## How to Cast Your Vote

### Step 1: Open the Vote

1. Navigate to the **Voting Center** from the drawer menu
2. Find the vote in the **Active** tab
3. Tap the vote card to open the detail screen

### Step 2: Review the Questions

Each vote contains one or more questions. For each question, you'll see:

* The question title and description
* The available options
* The option type (single choice, multiple choice, or Yes/No/Abstain)

### Step 3: Make Your Selections

Depending on the option type:

**Single Choice:**
* Tap one option to select it
* Tap a different option to change your selection

**Multiple Choice:**
* Tap options to select or deselect them
* The question may specify minimum and/or maximum selections required
* A counter shows how many selections you've made (e.g., "Select 2 to 4")

**Yes/No/Abstain:**
* Choose **Yes**, **No**, or **Abstain**
* Exactly one selection is required

### Step 4: Submit Your Vote

1. After making selections for all questions, tap **Submit Vote**
2. A confirmation dialog appears showing your selections
3. Review your choices carefully
4. Tap **Confirm** to submit

⚠️ **Important**: Once submitted, your vote **cannot be changed**. Review your selections carefully before confirming.

## After Voting

After successfully submitting your vote:

* A confirmation message appears
* Your voting status updates to "Voted"
* The timestamp of your vote is recorded
* For **polls** (non-anonymous), your selections are visible to managers
* For **ballots** (anonymous), your individual selections cannot be viewed by anyone

## Viewing Results

Depending on the vote configuration:

* **Real-time results enabled**: You can see running totals immediately after voting
* **Results after close only**: Results become visible only after the voting period ends
* **Managers only**: Results are visible only to vote managers

See [Vote Results](results.md) for more details.

## Weighted Voting

If the vote uses weighted voting:

* Your vote card shows your assigned vote weight
* If you are a proxy for absent delegates, your total weight includes their weights
* The proxy delegates assigned to you are listed on the vote detail screen

## Troubleshooting

### "Not Eligible" Message

You may see this if:
* You don't meet the financial standing requirements
* You haven't checked into the required meeting
* You're not in the vote's scope (chapter, province, or national)

Contact your chapter or the vote creator if you believe this is an error. Managers can manually add eligible voters.

### Vote Not Appearing

* Pull down to refresh the vote list
* Check that you're viewing the correct tab (Active, Completed, or Drafts)
* The vote may be scoped to a different level than you're currently viewing

### Submit Button Disabled

* Make sure you've made selections for **all** questions
* For multiple choice, ensure you meet the minimum selection requirement
