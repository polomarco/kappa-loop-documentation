# Joining a Chapter

You can request to join additional chapters as an associate member, or request a primary chapter transfer.

## How to Submit a Join Request

### From Chapter Details

1. Navigate to the chapter you want to join (via **Browse Chapters** or **Find Chapters**)
2. Open the chapter detail screen
3. Tap the **Join Chapter** button
4. Fill out the join request form

### From My Chapters

1. Open **My Chapters** from the drawer menu
2. Tap **Find Chapters**
3. Search for and select the chapter
4. Tap **Join Chapter**

## Join Request Form

When requesting to join a chapter, you'll provide:

* **Membership Type** - Choose "Primary" (chapter transfer) or "Associate" (additional membership)
* **Reason** _(required)_ - Explain why you'd like to join this chapter

⚠️ **Note**: Requesting a **Primary** membership change means transferring your home chapter. You can only have one primary chapter.

## Request Approval Process

After submitting a request:

1. Your request appears as **Pending** in your My Chapters screen
2. Chapter managers receive a notification about the new request
3. A chapter manager reviews your request
4. The manager can **Approve** or **Reject** the request
5. You'll see the updated status in your My Chapters screen

### Approval

If approved:
* Your new chapter membership is activated
* The chapter appears in your My Chapters list
* For primary transfers, your home chapter is updated

### Rejection

If rejected:
* The request status changes to "Rejected"
* Review notes from the manager may be provided
* You can submit a new request if circumstances change

## Viewing Request Status

Check the status of your requests in **My Chapters**:

* **Pending** - Awaiting review by a chapter manager
* **Approved** - Your membership has been granted
* **Rejected** - Your request was declined (check review notes for details)

## Membership Types

### Primary Membership

* Your main chapter affiliation
* Only one primary chapter at a time
* Determines your "home" chapter in the app

### Associate Membership

* Additional chapter affiliations
* You can be an associate member of multiple chapters
* Allows participation in multiple chapter activities

## Troubleshooting

### Join Button Not Visible

* You may already be a member of this chapter
* You may have a pending request for this chapter
* Check your My Chapters screen for existing memberships

### Request Taking Too Long

* Requests require manual review by a chapter manager
* Contact the chapter's Polemarch or Keeper of Records if your request hasn't been reviewed
