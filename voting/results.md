# Vote Results

Understand how to view and interpret vote outcomes, participation statistics, and detailed reports.

## Viewing Results

### When Results Are Available

Results availability depends on the vote configuration:

* **Real-time results**: Results are visible as votes are cast (before the vote closes)
* **After close**: Results appear only after the voting period ends
* **Managers only**: Only vote managers can view results

### Accessing Results

1. Open the vote from the **Voting Center**
2. Tap the vote card to view details
3. Scroll to the **Results** section (if available)

## Understanding Results

### Question Results

For each question in the vote, results display:

* **Option labels** with vote counts and percentages
* **Progress bars** showing the relative distribution of votes
* **Winning option** highlighted (the option with the most votes)
* **Total responses** for the question

### Participation Statistics

The results summary shows:

| Statistic | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Total Votes Cast** | Number of members who submitted votes |
| **Eligible Voters** | Total number of members eligible to vote |
| **Turnout Percentage** | Percentage of eligible voters who participated |
| **Time Closed** | When the voting period ended |

### Ballot vs. Poll Results

**Ballots (Anonymous):**
* Only aggregate counts and percentages are shown
* Individual voter selections are never revealed
* No voter names appear in results

**Polls (Non-Anonymous):**
* Aggregate counts and percentages are shown
* Managers can see which members selected which options
* Voter names may appear alongside their selections

## Weighted Vote Results

For votes using weighted voting (delegate lists):

* **Vote counts** show both raw vote count and weighted totals
* **Weight turnout** shows the percentage of total weight that was cast
* **Weighted percentages** reflect the proportional weight, not just head count

## Vote Reports _(Managers Only)_

Managers have access to detailed vote reports:

### Participation Report

Shows voting participation broken down by organizational unit:

* **By Province** - Turnout rates for each province
* **By Chapter** - Turnout rates for each chapter within a province
* **By Delegate** - Individual delegate voting status (for weighted votes)

Each level shows:
* Total delegates / voters
* Number who voted
* Turnout percentage
* Weight statistics (for weighted votes)

### Delegate Breakdown

For weighted votes, the report includes:

* Each delegate's membership number and name
* Chapter affiliation
* Vote weight
* Whether they voted (and when)
* Whether the vote was cast directly or by proxy
* The proxy holder (if applicable)

### Awardees Section

If the delegate list includes awardees (non-chapter delegates):
* Listed separately from chapter delegates
* Individual voting status and weight information

## Result Visibility Settings

Vote creators can configure who sees results:

| Setting | Who Can See Results |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Everyone** | All voters can see results after voting closes |
| **Managers Only** | Only vote managers can view results |
