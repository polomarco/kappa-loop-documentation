# My Chapters

The My Chapters screen shows all your chapter memberships and pending membership requests.

## Accessing My Chapters

1. Tap the **menu icon** (☰) in the top-left corner
2. Select **My Chapters** from the drawer menu

## Chapter Memberships

Your chapter memberships are displayed as cards showing:

* **Chapter name**
* **Chapter type** (Alumni or Undergraduate)
* **Region/Province** the chapter belongs to
* **Membership type** - Primary or Associate
* **Status** - Active, Pending, or Inactive
* **Join date**

### Primary vs. Associate Membership

* **Primary** - Your home chapter. You can only have one primary chapter.
* **Associate** - Additional chapter affiliations. You can be an associate member of multiple chapters.

## Pending Requests

Below your active memberships, you'll see any pending membership requests:

* **Chapter name** and type
* **Request status** - Pending, Approved, or Rejected
* **Membership type requested** (Primary or Associate)
* **Reason** you provided for joining
* **Submitted date**
* **Review notes** (if the request was reviewed)

## Finding and Joining New Chapters

From the My Chapters screen:

1. Tap the **Find Chapters** button
2. A search sheet opens where you can search for chapters
3. Select a chapter to view its details
4. Request to join from the chapter detail screen

See [Joining a Chapter](joining.md) for the complete process.

## Viewing Chapter Details

Tap any chapter membership card to navigate to that chapter's detail screen, where you can view chapter information, officers, and members.
