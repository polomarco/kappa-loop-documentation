# Meeting Management

Kappa Loop provides a full meeting management system for scheduling, tracking attendance, and managing chapter and province meetings.

## Overview

The meeting system supports:

* **Chapter Meetings** - Regular chapter meetings and special sessions
* **Province Meetings** - Province-level meetings and conferences
* **Multiple Formats** - Virtual, in-person, and hybrid meetings
* **Attendance Tracking** - Check-in/check-out with role assignments
* **Meeting Materials** - Attach agendas, minutes, presentations, and other documents

## Key Features

* **Meeting Scheduling** - Create and manage meetings with date, time, and location
* **Check-in System** - Digital attendance tracking with QR code and manual check-in
* **Attendee Management** - View attendees, roles, and attendance status
* **Virtual Meeting Support** - Integration with Zoom, Google Meet, and other platforms
* **Meeting Attachments** - Upload and share meeting materials
* **Recording Support** - Link meeting recordings for later access

## Meeting Types

* **Chapter Meeting** - Regular chapter meetings
* **Province Meeting** - Province-level meetings

## Meeting Formats

| Format | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Virtual** | Online meeting with meeting link, platform, and optional passcode |
| **In-Person** | Physical meeting with location name, address, and room number |
| **Hybrid** | Both virtual and in-person options available |

## Meeting Statuses

* **Scheduled** - Meeting is planned for a future date
* **In Progress** - Meeting is currently taking place
* **Completed** - Meeting has concluded
* **Cancelled** - Meeting was cancelled (with optional cancellation reason)

## Invitation Scopes

When creating a meeting, the organizer can set who is invited:

* **All Chapter Members** - Every member of the chapter can see and join
* **Chapter Officers Only** - Only chapter officers are invited
* **Specific Members** - Only selected members receive invitations
