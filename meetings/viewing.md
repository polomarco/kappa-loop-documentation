# Viewing Meetings

Browse and view details for chapter and province meetings.

## Accessing Meetings

Meetings are accessible from the chapter or province screen:

1. Navigate to your **Chapter** screen (via FAB → Chapter) or **Province** screen (via FAB → Province)
2. Look for the **Meetings** section or tab
3. Browse upcoming and past meetings

## Meeting List

Meetings are organized by status:

### Upcoming Meetings

* Meetings scheduled for the future
* Sorted by date (soonest first)
* Shows meeting title, date, time, and format

### In-Progress Meetings

* Meetings currently taking place
* Check-in may be available
* Highlighted for easy identification

### Past Meetings

* Completed meetings
* Attendance records and attachments available
* Recording links (if available)

## Meeting Card Information

Each meeting card displays:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Title** | Meeting name |
| **Date & Time** | Start and end times with timezone |
| **Format** | Virtual, In-Person, or Hybrid badge |
| **Status** | Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled |
| **Location** | Physical address or "Virtual" indicator |

## Meeting Detail Screen

Tap a meeting card to view the full details. The detail screen has two tabs:

### Details Tab

**Meeting Information:**
* Title, description, and meeting type
* Date, start time, and end time
* Timezone
* Format (Virtual/In-Person/Hybrid)
* Status

**Location Details** (for in-person/hybrid):
* Location name
* Street address, city, state, zip code
* Room number (if specified)

**Virtual Meeting Details** (for virtual/hybrid):
* Meeting platform (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.)
* Meeting link (tappable to join)
* Meeting ID
* Passcode

**Recording:**
* Recording URL (if recording is available, tappable to view)

**Cancellation:**
* If the meeting is cancelled, the cancellation reason is displayed

### Attachments Tab

View and download meeting materials:

* **Agendas** - Meeting agenda documents
* **Minutes** - Meeting minutes and notes
* **Presentations** - Slide decks and presentation files
* **Handouts** - Reference materials for attendees
* **Other** - Any additional documents

Supported file types include PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, and videos.

Tap an attachment to:
* Preview it in-app (for supported formats)
* Download and open in an external app

## Quick Actions

Depending on your role and the meeting status, you may see action buttons:

* **Join Meeting** - Open the virtual meeting link (for virtual/hybrid meetings)
* **Check In** - Open the attendance check-in screen (for in-progress meetings)
* **View Attendees** - See the full attendee list
* **Edit Meeting** - Modify meeting details (for meeting organizers)
* **Upload Attachment** - Add meeting materials (for authorized users)
* **Export Attendance** - Download attendance records (for meeting organizers)

## Navigating Back

Tap the back arrow to return to the chapter or province screen.
